Bio

Tsung-Hsien (Shawn) Wen is a co-founder and CTO of PolyAI, a London company that builds voice AI agents that answer customer calls for enterprises. PolyAI recently released Dialog-RSN-1, an audio-native model that hears the caller directly. PolyAI has raised more than $200M in venture funding.

Before PolyAI, Shawn worked as a research scientist at Google Brain and a conversational AI consultant at IPsoft Amelia. He holds a PhD from the Dialogue Systems Group, University of Cambridge, where he worked with Professor Steve Young. His research on conversational AI won best paper awards at EMNLP 2015 and SigDial 2015. He hosted the first two workshops on NLP for Conversational AI and chaired the conversational AI track at ACL and EMNLP.
